Baller Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

Baller is an Android app with home-screen widgets and a screensaver that show confident, funny affirmations and let you keep a tally of your wins. It is made by Next Lifetime Ventures LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Baller has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not request the internet permission, so nothing is ever sent to us or to anyone else. The only data that moves at all is the optional watch sync described below, between your own phone and watch.

What stays on your device

The app stores a few things locally so it works: your check-in answer (which voice packs you want), your language and screensaver settings, and the wins you log, including any short notes you type and the date of each win. All of it stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's own device backup may include it if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Watch sync

If you use Baller on a Wear OS watch, your phone and watch keep each other up to date: your check-in answer, language and look settings, your win counts and streak go from phone to watch, and wins you log on the watch go to your phone. This uses Google's Wear OS Data Layer, which connects your own paired devices (usually directly over Bluetooth). We never receive any of it. Uninstalling the app removes it from both devices.

Children

Baller is rated for mature audiences and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

No health claims

Baller is for fun and motivation. It is not a medical or mental-health product and does not give medical advice.
