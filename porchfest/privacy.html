Savannah Porchfest Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

This policy applies to the Savannah Porchfest apps for iPhone and Android and the Porchfest web app.

The short version

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your plan, likes and porch passport stay on your phone. If you turn on notifications, we store a push token and the IDs of the sets you saved, only so festival announcements reach you. If you sign up to volunteer, or apply to play, host a porch, sell or sponsor, we keep the contact details you give us so the festival can organise it.

What stays on your phone

  • The sets you save to My Day, the genres you like, your pace, your reminder setting and your porch passport stamps.
  • A copy of the festival schedule, so the app works without a signal.
  • Show reminders and "did you enjoy the show?" Tip Jar reminders are scheduled on your phone. They are not sent from our server.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What we receive, and why

  • If you allow notifications: an anonymous push token for your device, your platform (iOS or Android), and the IDs of the sets in your plan. Producers use this to send announcements to everyone (for example a rain delay), or only to people who saved an affected set. The token can't identify you, and we don't receive your name, email, phone number or location.
  • If you sign up to volunteer: your name, the email and/or phone number you give, the areas you'd like to help with and any note you add. Only festival producers can see them, in the producer dashboard. We use them only to contact you about volunteering, never for marketing, and we don't share them. They're kept with that year's festival records and removed from the dashboard when the next festival year starts. Ask us at any time to delete them.
  • If you apply to play, host a porch, be a vendor or sponsor: your name, email and/or phone, and your answers (for a porch host, the porch's address; for a band, its links and Tip Jar). Only festival producers see your contact details. If you're accepted, your band, porch, business or sponsorship is listed in the app; a porch's address is shown only once music is scheduled there, and your contact details are never shown. Applications are kept with that year's festival records and removed from the dashboard when the next festival year starts; the contact details of accepted participants stay with their listing so producers can reach them, until you ask us to remove them.
  • If you enter the raffle: when your porch passport is full on festival day, the app sends a random raffle number made up on your phone (like K7Q-4MZ), the porch numbers you checked in at, and your platform. There's no name, email or phone number, so the number can't be traced to you. We use it only to draw winners, and we delete entries before the next festival.
  • If you scan the Porchfest app QR code: we count the scan: the day, whether it was an iPhone, Android or other device, and which printed code it was. Nothing that identifies you is kept.
  • When the app loads the schedule: our server sees a normal web request, including your IP address, like any website. We don't use it to identify or profile anyone.

Push messages are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google's notification systems. We don't sell or share data with anyone else, and we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs.

Links to other services

Buttons such as Tip Jar (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal), YouTube, Apple Music, band websites and Directions open those services, under their own privacy policies. Tips go straight to the band. The festival never handles your payment.

Your choices

  • Turn notifications off in the app (My Day, Show alerts) or in your phone's Settings. Push tokens we haven't been able to reach are deleted automatically.
  • To ask us to delete the push token for your device, email us with the approximate date you used the app.
  • To see, correct or delete your volunteer sign-up or application, email us from the address you used, or tell a festival producer.

Children

Porchfest is an all-ages event. The app doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Volunteer sign-up asks people to confirm they're 18 or older, or that a parent or guardian is signing them up.
