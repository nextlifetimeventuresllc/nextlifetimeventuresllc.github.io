QiTone Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

QiTone is a music and sound app for Android and iPhone, made by Next Lifetime Ventures LLC. It has no account, no sign-in and no advertising. This page says plainly what the app keeps on your phone, the little that leaves it, and what never does.

What stays on your device

  • Your birth date, time and place, if you choose to enter them. They are used on the phone to work out your Stems & Branches profile and are never sent to us.
  • Your playlists, the look you chose and your settings.
  • Which feelings you have chosen and whether a session helped, which the app uses to adjust later sessions for you.

These are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Your phone's own backup (Google or Apple) may include them if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google or Apple under your account with them, not by us, and we cannot see it.

What the app sends

Anonymous play totals. The app sends plain counts: which piece of music was played, how many times, on which day, and which kind of session was started. This is how the musicians who make the music are paid and how we learn which music is used.

These counts carry no name, no email address, no account, no device or advertising identifier, no location and none of your birth details. We cannot tell which person or which phone a count came from. You can turn this off at any time in the app under You → Share play counts.

Music downloads. The app downloads its music from our storage at Google Firebase. As with any internet connection, the servers that deliver the files and receive the counts see your internet address while doing so. We do not use it to identify you.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe, the purchase is made through your Google Play or App Store account. Google or Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, your card or your address; the app is only told whether a subscription is active.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. We do not hold any to sell.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or location.

Musicians

Musicians invited to make music for QiTone sign in to a separate web page with an email address and password. We keep that email address, the name they wish to be credited by, the music they send and their play totals, in order to credit and pay them. A musician can ask us to remove their account at the address below.

Deleting your information

Everything personal is on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. Because the play totals are not linked to you, there is nothing of yours for us to look up or delete.

Children

QiTone is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Health note

QiTone is intended for relaxation, mindfulness, wellness, and educational use. It is not a medical device, is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: dev@nextlifetimeventuresllc.com.
