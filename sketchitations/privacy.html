Sketchitations Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

Sketchitations is a pass-and-play drawing game for Android phones and tablets, made by Next Lifetime Ventures LLC. The short version: the app doesn't collect, send or sell any of your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Sketchitations has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything about you or your games anywhere.

What stays on your device

To let you pause and resume a game, Sketchitations saves the current game on your device only. That includes the player names you type in, the phrases and guesses, and the drawings. It is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read.

The saved game is replaced when you start a new one, and you can remove it at any time with "Discard saved game" on the home screen. Uninstalling the app deletes it from your device.

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include the saved game in that backup. The backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Sharing

When you tap Share after a reveal, Sketchitations creates an image of that chain and hands it to Android's share menu. You choose where it goes, for example a messaging or social media app. Once shared, the image is handled by the app you chose and its own privacy policy. Sketchitations does not share anything unless you tap Share.

Children

Sketchitations is designed to be family-friendly and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no information at all, there is nothing for us to access, review or delete. Parents who want to clear a saved game can use "Discard saved game" or uninstall the app.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds features that work over the internet, such as playing with friends on other devices, this policy will be updated before those features are released, with a new effective date. Questions: nextlifetimeventuresllc@gmail.com.
