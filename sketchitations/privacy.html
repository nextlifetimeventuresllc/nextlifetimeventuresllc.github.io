Sketchitations Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

Sketchitations is a drawing game for Android phones and tablets, made by Next Lifetime Ventures LLC, with two ways to play. Pass-and-play runs entirely on your device and collects nothing. Online play is optional and stores a small amount of information on our servers so that people on different devices can play together. We don’t show ads, we don’t track you, and we don’t sell information.

Pass-and-play

Nothing leaves your device. To let you pause and resume, the current game (the player names you type, the phrases, guesses and drawings) is saved in the app’s private storage. It is replaced when you start a new game, and you can remove it with “Discard saved game” on the home screen or by uninstalling the app.

If Android’s device backup is turned on, Android may include the saved game in that backup. The backup is handled by Google under your Google account.

Online play

Online play is off until you tap Play online. When you do, we store:

  • A random player ID. Created automatically. It is not your name, email address or phone number, and we don’t ask for any of those.
  • The display name you type. Other players in your games see it. A first name or nickname is all you need.
  • What you make in online games: the phrases, guesses and drawings. The other players in the same game see them.
  • Your saved and blocked players. Only you can see these lists.
  • A notification token for your device, so we can tell you when it’s your turn. Notifications only appear if you allow them.
  • Reports you send about another player, with the game they relate to.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos, email address, phone number or advertising ID.

Where it’s kept, and for how long

Online play runs on Firebase, a service from Google, which stores this information for us on Google’s servers and delivers notifications. Google handles it under the Firebase privacy terms.

  • Online games and everything in them are deleted automatically 30 days after the game is created.
  • Your display name and your saved and blocked lists are kept until you delete them.
  • Reports are kept for as long as we need them to keep play safe.

Deleting your online data

In the app: Play online → Saved and blocked players → Delete my online data. That removes your display name, your lists and your player ID. Games you were in are removed on the 30-day schedule above. You can also email us at the address below and we will delete it for you.

Playing with people you don’t know

“Play with anyone” matches you with other players. They see your display name and what you write and draw in that game, and you see theirs. You can block or report any player; a blocked player is not matched with you again and can’t join or invite you to games. We review reports and may remove players who break the rules. A player who is blocked by many different people is removed from online play automatically.

Sharing

When you tap Share after a reveal, the app makes an image of that chain and hands it to Android’s share menu. You choose where it goes. Once shared, the image is handled by the app you chose and its own privacy policy.

Children

Pass-and-play collects nothing from anyone. Online play with friends is meant for people who know each other; children should use it only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. “Play with anyone” is for ages 13 and up. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond the display name and game content described above. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete a child’s online data at any time, in the app or by email.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Questions: nextlifetimeventuresllc@gmail.com.
