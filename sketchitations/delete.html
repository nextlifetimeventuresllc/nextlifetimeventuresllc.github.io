Delete your Sketchitations data

Sketchitations has no accounts with emails or passwords. If you have used online play, we hold a small amount of data under an automatic player ID. You can delete it yourself in the app, or ask us to.

Delete it in the app

  • Open Sketchitations and tap Play online.
  • Tap Your saved players, blocks and settings.
  • Tap Delete my online data, then Delete.

Or ask us

Email nextlifetimeventuresllc@gmail.com from any address and tell us the display name you use in the game. We will delete your data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your display name
  • Your saved players and blocked players lists
  • Notes and invites waiting for you
  • Your notification token and device identifier
  • Your player ID

What is kept, and for how long

  • Games you played in (the drawings and phrases, with the display name you used) are deleted automatically 30 days after the game was created.
  • Games that another player reported are kept for up to one year so the report can be reviewed.
  • Reports and bans are kept for as long as needed to keep online play safe. A ban stays in place after deletion.

Pass-and-play games are never sent to us. They are stored only on your device and are removed when you discard the saved game or uninstall the app.
