Telephone, but with drawings

Sketchitations

One player writes a phrase. The next sees only the words and draws them. The next sees only the drawing and guesses what it is. Draw, guess, draw, guess, down the line. Then everyone watches the reveal and sees what the message turned into.

Pass one phone or tablet around the group; that works offline, so it’s made for theme park lines, road trips and waiting rooms. Or play online: send friends a game code and everyone plays on their own phone, or get matched with other players. Funniest with 6 to 10 players.

Turn timers, silly dares for more time, seven family-friendly idea packs, and a reveal you can share.

When it’s handy

  • Long drives. Pass one phone or tablet around the passengers. It works with no signal. (The driver sits this one out.)
  • Waiting. For a table, for food, in a theme park line, at the airport gate. A quick round with three or four people takes a few minutes and pauses whenever you need to move.
  • Family game night. Anyone who can write a short phrase and doodle can play, so parents, grandparents and kids can play together. The built-in ideas are family-friendly.
  • Parties and get-togethers. Funniest with 6 to 10 people, when the message has the most room to drift.
  • Friends who aren’t in the same place. Play online, each on your own phone, live or across the day with a notification when it’s your turn. Online play is for ages 13 and up.

Good to know

  • 3 to 12 players. Two people is too few for the message to change.
  • For iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets. iPhone and Android players can play online together.
