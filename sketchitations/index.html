Telephone, but with drawings

Sketchitations

One player writes a phrase. The next sees only the words and draws them. The next sees only the drawing and guesses what it is. Draw, guess, draw, guess, down the line. Then everyone watches the reveal and sees what the message turned into.

Pass one phone or tablet around the group. It works offline, so it's made for theme park lines, road trips and waiting rooms. Funniest with 6 to 10 players.

Turn timers, silly dares for more time, seven family-friendly idea packs, and a reveal you can share.
