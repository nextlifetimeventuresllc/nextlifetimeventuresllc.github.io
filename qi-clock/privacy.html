Qi Clock Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026 · Next Lifetime Ventures LLC

Qi Clock is an Android app, home-screen widget, screensaver and Wear OS watch face that shows the traditional Chinese organ (meridian) clock. It is made by Next Lifetime Ventures LLC.

What we collect

Nothing. Qi Clock has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

What stays on your device

The app reads your device's clock, date and time zone to show the current organ window and season, and it remembers your settings: the art style you chose and your seasonal preferences. These are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Children

Qi Clock is a general-audience app and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Health note

The organ clock is a traditional reflective framework from Chinese medicine. Qi Clock is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: nextlifetimeventuresllc@gmail.com.
